Where do I find the cookie?
  1. Log in at instagram.com in your browser.
  2. Open DevTools (F12) → Application (Chrome/Edge) or Storage (Firefox) → Cookies → https://www.instagram.com.
  3. Copy the value of sessionid and paste it above. Saving checks it with a single request first.

Unofficial: automated access is against Instagram's Terms of Use. This fetches once a day, one page of 50 at a time with pauses, and stops at the first sign of trouble. If Instagram logs the session out, the report says so and you paste a fresh cookie here.
