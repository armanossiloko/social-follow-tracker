Where do I find the cookie?
  1. Log in at instagram.com in your browser.
  2. Press F12, then go to Application (Chrome/Edge) or Storage (Firefox) → Cookies → https://www.instagram.com.
  3. Copy the value of sessionid and paste it above.

This uses Instagram unofficially, which is against its Terms of Use. It fetches once a day, slowly. If Instagram logs the session out, paste a fresh cookie here.
