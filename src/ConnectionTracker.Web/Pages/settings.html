Facebook

{{facebook_status}}

Facebook has no API for friends lists, so its data comes from the official export. Each export is a snapshot; comparing two shows who is no longer your friend.

How to get an export
  1. On Facebook, open Settings & privacy → Settings → Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Download your information.
  2. Pick your Facebook profile, then Some of your information and tick Friends.
  3. Choose Download to device, date range All time, format JSON.
  4. When it's ready, upload the zip under Import an export.
